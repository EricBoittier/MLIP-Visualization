Velocity Verlet at constant particle number, volume and energy, with forces from the model in the browser. A good potential and time step keep the total energy flat while kinetic and potential energy trade places. Starting from a relaxed structure, about half the initial kinetic energy flows into the potential, so the temperature settles near T₀/2. Direct forces are not the gradient of any energy, so the total drifts. Too large a Δt does the same, faster.

Diffusion Monte Carlo treats the nuclei as quantum particles. The structure is relaxed on the model's surface first. Then walkers, each a copy of the molecule, take random steps sized by ħ²Δτ/m. Each walker is copied or removed with weight exp(−Δτ (V − Eref)), and Eref is steered to keep the population steady. The population settles into the vibrational ground state, and the mean of Eref above the minimum is the zero-point energy. The cloud shows where the walkers are (light atoms spread furthest). Only energies are needed, so every walker is evaluated in batched passes. Walkers that fall far below the minimum have found a hole in the surface and are removed.

FLOPs are estimated from the shapes of the kernels the model runs (a multiply-add counts as 2). The rate includes everything else in a step: the neighbour list, data transfer and the read-back of results.
