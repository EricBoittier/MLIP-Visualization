Velocity Verlet at constant particle number, volume and energy, with forces from the model in the browser. A good potential and time step keep the total energy flat while kinetic and potential energy trade places. Starting from a relaxed structure, about half the initial kinetic energy flows into the potential, so the temperature settles near T₀/2. Direct forces are not the gradient of any energy, so the total drifts. Too large a Δt does the same, faster.
